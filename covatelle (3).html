🎀 Welcome back, Baker!

Scan receipts from Shopee, Robinsons, or any store — CovaTelle auto-reads the item name, quantity & price, organizes them in an Excel-style ledger with photos, barcodes, PD & expiration, computes your food costing, at may Orders Calendar para sa bookings mo — lahat naka-sync realtime. ✨

☁️ Auto Cloud Sync

Naka-sync automatically sa cloud — kahit anong device, buksan mo lang ito at updated na lahat. Walang Workspace ID needed, walang mawawalang data. 💾✨

📤 Export to Excel

Download your whole ledger anytime — receipts, ingredients, costing — as .xlsx files you can open in Excel / Google Sheets. 📊
